DriVerdict Beta Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current DriVerdict private beta handles screen analysis, ride-request information, diagnostics, and local app data.

Purpose

DriVerdict is an Android ride-request analysis tool for rideshare drivers. It helps estimate whether a supported ride request meets your standards by calculating hourly value, per-mile value, and a GO, MAYBE, or NO GO result.

Screen Capture and OCR

When Real-Time Mode is active, DriVerdict uses Android screen analysis only after you approve the Android permission prompt for that session. DriVerdict reads visible screen frames so it can identify and analyze supported ride-request screens.

DriVerdict performs text recognition on the device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition. DriVerdict does not create or save a screen-recording video.

In the current beta, DriVerdict does not upload screenshots, screen frames, OCR images, or OCR text to DriVerdict-operated servers.

Ride-Request Information

DriVerdict may process ride-request details visible on supported screens, including fare, pickup time, pickup distance, trip time, trip distance, platform label, other visible request text where applicable, calculated hourly value, calculated per-mile value, and the resulting GO, MAYBE, or NO GO decision.

The current beta keeps same-day dashboard aggregates locally, such as offer counts, decision counts, and value totals by platform. DriVerdict does not maintain a full persistent ride history in the current beta.

Location

The current beta does not request precise location, approximate location, or background location access.

Local Diagnostics

DriVerdict may store local diagnostic logs to help troubleshoot beta issues. These logs may include raw and normalized OCR text, calculated ride values, parser details, lifecycle and recovery events, app version, device model, Android version, memory details, screen-analysis status, and related troubleshooting information.

Local daily diagnostic log files are cleaned up after approximately 30 days.

DriVerdict includes a diagnostic capability for OCR PNG and TXT artifacts, but that artifact saving is disabled in the current beta.

Diagnostic Sharing

The current beta does not automatically upload diagnostic logs and does not include an in-app diagnostic sharing path. If you manually send diagnostic files to support, review them first and remove or avoid sending passenger names, complete pickup or dropoff addresses, account credentials, private screenshots, or other unnecessary personal information.

Preferences and Local Storage

DriVerdict stores local preferences and operating state, including onboarding completion, decision thresholds, diagnostic logging mode, Real-Time Mode recovery state, overlay position, process session identifiers, a short lifecycle event buffer, and same-day dashboard summary values.

Android Backup and Device Transfer

DriVerdict stores diagnostics, ride-analysis information, preferences, and dashboard statistics locally on the device. DriVerdict is configured to prevent its app data from being included in Android cloud backups or Android device-to-device transfer extraction.

Third-Party Services and SDKs

The current beta uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition for on-device OCR. The current beta does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or DriVerdict telemetry SDKs.

Data Retention and Deletion

Same-day dashboard aggregates reset by date. Local diagnostic logs are cleaned up after approximately 30 days. You can also remove locally stored DriVerdict data by clearing app storage or uninstalling DriVerdict through Android settings.

Android Permissions

DriVerdict uses display-over-other-apps permission to show the result overlay, foreground-service capability so Real-Time Mode can continue while active, and Android screen analysis permission for user-authorized screen capture during an active Real-Time Mode session.

Security Limitations

DriVerdict stores beta diagnostics and app data locally on your device. Device security, screenshots taken outside DriVerdict, files you manually share, and manufacturer-specific transfer behavior outside standard Android backup and extraction settings may be outside DriVerdict's control.

Children

DriVerdict is intended for rideshare drivers and is not directed to children.

Future Changes

DriVerdict may introduce additional optional features in the future. If a new feature materially changes how information is collected or used, this policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before the feature is enabled.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support.tripiq@gmail.com.
